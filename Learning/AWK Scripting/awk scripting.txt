awk scripting:
               awk 
                   'BEGIN{start-action}                      #action before file read
                          pattern/condition{action}
                    END{stop-action}'                        #action after file read
                    filename
ex:    
awk 'BEGIN{print"-------welcome-------"} {print$0} END{print-----go now-----}' rec.txt

we can use programing concepts in it:
       -variables
       -if/else
       -array
       -for loop
       -while loop
       -functions

-find sum of salary: 
          awk 'BEGIN{sum=0} {sum=sum+$NF} END{print "sum of salary: "sum}' rec.txt    
                                                # it will read all the lines one by one and sum the total salary
-total numbers of empolyees and remove header and average of salary:
          awk '$NR>1 {if($NF>0)c++; sum+=$NF} END{print "Total Empolyees are:" c, "Total salaries:" sum, "average of salary: sum/c"}' rec.txt 
-show if the salary is larger then 1000:
          awk '{if($NF>100)$7="HIGH"; print$0}' rec.txt
-show total salary of a department:
          awk '{if($3=="HR"s+=$NF)} END{print "Total salry of HR dep is: "s}' rec.
